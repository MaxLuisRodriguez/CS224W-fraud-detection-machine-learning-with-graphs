# Research decision — September 24, 2026

**Decision:** study avoidable evidence-search failure under ordinary transaction-graph growth, with shared-account reasoning as the mechanism to test. The [active plan](PROJECT_PLAN.md) replaces both the original acquisition-efficiency proposal and the later adversarial-distraction proposal.

## Why the supplied audit changes the plan

A legal successful replay shows retrievability. It does not show that observations allowed an investigator to identify the successful route. The new core comparison is with an optimal policy that knows the declared graph distribution but does not know the realized hidden graph.

The contribution is analysis first. A graph model is an experimental instrument; learned methods follow only if strong simple and nonmyopic competitors leave meaningful headroom. Planning under partial graph observations and acquiring structured evidence have close precedents, including selective network discovery, hierarchical acquisition, ACO, and PEGE. The reference and regret equations are standard tools, not asserted new results.

## Concrete changes

- Replace the initial attacker/defense study with ordinary growth and informative/null relational conditions.
- Replace successful-trace imitation with posterior expected action values on training distributions.
- Put a symbolic verifier and exact observation-constrained planner before GNN training.
- Compare identity-aware models with equal information; label identity removal as an information intervention.
- Add clean-only versus growth-augmented training for both architectures.
- Set a three-edge-per-path witness and primary budget six. Its five-query minimum makes the audit's proposed four-call budget an impossibility control rather than the main learning condition.
- Allocate 44 hours to a completed controlled pilot; remove full AML ingestion, a dashboard, attacks, and stopping from its critical path.
- Gate AML work on historical evidence sufficiency before investing in acquisition training.
- Preserve the main-conference ambition while making continuation depend on mechanism, baseline, and transfer results.

## Deferred alternatives

Policy-shift-aware abstention and adversarial distraction remain possible later projects, not parallel objectives. No claim about an actual Socratix weakness is made. A simple-method win must be accepted rather than countered by manufacturing harder distractors.
