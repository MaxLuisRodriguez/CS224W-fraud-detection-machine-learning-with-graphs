# Research decision — September 25, 2026

**Decision:** pivot from single-case shared-account witness discovery to joint evidence acquisition across related fraud alerts. The [active plan](PROJECT_PLAN.md) is the governing proposal.

## Audit findings

The previous diamond example exposed shared identity only when the target pattern was complete. It did not establish a useful pre-decision cue. A graph motif also did not supply a money-laundering label. An optimal tiny-world reference and a GNN do not by themselves constitute a main-track contribution.

The new setting makes shared identity actionable before acquisition: related alerts already name common accounts, or become connected through retrieved records. A request can improve multiple predictions. The question is whether joint sequential valuation beats case-local policies with the same cache and strong global acquisition/planning methods.

## Literature constraints

Collective label acquisition (KDD 2008), nonmyopic value of information (IJCAI 2005), cohort modality acquisition (CAMA), ACO, and PEGE are material overlaps. Public primary sources were checked during this revision. The plan states inspection scope and does not claim exhaustive novelty clearance or a reproduced result. One newly found preprint also warns empirically that adding graph/agent components need not improve fraud detection.

Global budget allocation, evidence reuse, caching, and Bellman value learning are not new by themselves. The new plan therefore requires a specific methodological advantage and independent evidence rather than treating the setting's name as the contribution.

## New constraints and choices

- The user supplied a $20,000 AWS/Azure budget. Interpret it conservatively as combined, superseding the earlier $300 assumption.
- Plan a 50-hour prototype and a separate 20–24-week research program.
- Reserve $5,000; gate major experiments on the first $2,000/$4,000 feasibility and baseline stages.
- Use explicit retrospective review times rather than silently allowing post-target records.
- Give all substantive methods identical global evidence and caching; report bulk-query controls.
- Use supplied AML labels as the financial prediction target; no motif is declared proof of crime.
- Treat the proposed joint continuation-value model as a candidate, not an established novel algorithm.
- Keep independent-generator and nonfinancial generalization, with access and label checks.
